## Comparison to E01-R Precision

**A. What now happens to the skin?** The shader adds a bounded, localized brightness and edge/contrast response around the energy path, with a small refracted sample. In the captures this response remains too faint to read as a treatment event; the strict identity frame mostly reads as the original portrait.

**B. What creates depth?** A shallow offset sample follows local image contrast, with modest edge separation. It does not yet create a convincing sense of energy acting beneath the surface.

**C. Why is the energy controlled rather than decorative?** Its path and focal region are bounded and progress-driven rather than random. However, the field is not perceptually strong enough to establish precision without the labels.

**D. How does technology now belong to the scene?** The unchanged Argon product asset is composited in the same GPU pass and receives scene luminance and a contact shadow. The evidence frame still reads too much like a cutout over the subject, so integration fails.

**E. What remains when all explanatory evidence is removed?** An editorial portrait remains. The image does not yet communicate controlled energy, skin response, depth, and precision on its own.

## R2 conclusion

This renderer pass does not meet the Laser Signature, strict identity, or material transformation gates. The transition remains too close to one photographic material emerging through a soft field. Record this as `CURRENT_APPROACH_LIMIT_REACHED`; do not proceed to another polish iteration on this material/rendering approach.
