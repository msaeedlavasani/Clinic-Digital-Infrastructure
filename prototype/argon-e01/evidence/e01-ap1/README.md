# E01-AP1 — Laser Art Production Study

Three distinct, AI-generated 16:9 art-direction frames for Owner and independent Design Review. These are visual studies only: they are not Argon product photography, verified depictions of laser physics, or approved treatment claims. The optical and subsurface cues are experiential metaphors. No motion or E01 runtime integration is included.

## Direction A — Subsurface Light

**CONCEPT**  A localized optical event appears to enter the cheek and disperse within the skin.

**SKIN**  Close photographic cheek and jaw with visible natural texture; a contained luminous response sits beneath the surface impression.

**ENERGY**  A small, cool-white optical event from a cropped aperture, with no drawn beam or scan line.

**DEPTH**  The focal highlight and softened subsurface scatter suggest a shallow internal field against the sharply photographed skin.

**TECHNOLOGY**  A partial optical handpiece enters at the far edge; its aperture and light source share the scene and aim toward the treatment area.

**LUXURY**  Restrained cool palette, controlled crop, broad negative space, tactile skin, and one localized event.

**RISK**  The blue-white response could become a generic glow or make skin appear oily/over-retouched if amplified.

**MOTION_POTENTIAL**  The focal response could arrive, bloom just beneath the surface, and dissipate locally while the portrait remains still. Not implemented.

## Direction B — Optical Precision

**CONCEPT**  Precision is expressed through a crisp focal plane, a measured instrument-to-skin relationship, and highly selective sharpness.

**SKIN**  A natural three-quarter portrait remains the main subject; a single controlled highlight resolves surface texture.

**ENERGY**  No beam. A small, sharply bounded optical reflection and a subtle spectral edge at the aperture imply calibrated treatment energy.

**DEPTH**  The near-field handpiece, cheek focal plane, and soft background form three clear spatial layers.

**TECHNOLOGY**  A real-looking optical treatment head occupies the right foreground as part of the photographed scene, aligned toward but separated from the face.

**LUXURY**  Sculpted focus, restrained cool neutrals, and an intentional balance between living skin and precision optics.

**RISK**  The instrument is visually dominant and may drift into a product render; the bright cheek highlight may read as beauty makeup.

**MOTION_POTENTIAL**  Focus could settle from the lens to a small skin region; the localized reflection could resolve and hold. Not implemented.

## Direction C — Material Transformation

**CONCEPT**  Treatment is suggested through a gradual change in light, focus, and surface character rather than a visible energy event.

**SKIN**  Neck, jaw, and shoulder geometry stay human and photographic; the shoulder transitions from natural matte shadow to restrained luminosity.

**ENERGY**  No visible beam or spot. A nearby optical reflection and localized light-wrap imply a treatment interaction indirectly.

**DEPTH**  The shoulder/neck plane, softly defocused aperture reflection, and quiet background create spatial separation.

**TECHNOLOGY**  Optical glass is only indirectly present at the far edge as a photographed reflection; no device is displayed.

**LUXURY**  Airy mineral tones, sculptural human form, soft dimensional light, and an unhurried editorial crop.

**RISK**  Without a clearer medical-treatment cue, this may read as fragrance or fashion imagery rather than laser treatment.

**MOTION_POTENTIAL**  A small region of changed focus and luminosity could travel across the shoulder and settle back into natural texture. Not implemented.
